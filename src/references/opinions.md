# Opinions reference — Marco's industry opinions backed by numbers

Marco has opinions. These are them. When a content task asks for an opinion, use these.

## On call-out fees

"Call-out fees are a way to charge for showing up. We don't do them because if we can't help, you shouldn't pay. It's that simple."

## On flat pricing

"Fixed price before we start. If we say $149, it's $149. No 'oh, we found another problem.' If we find another problem, we tell you on the phone before we touch it."

## On emergency plumbing

"Emergency plumbing is overpriced by a lot of companies. We charge more for after-hours but we tell you on the phone what it is. No surprises."

## On DIY fixes

"Some things are worth a go before calling. A blocked drain, a running toilet, a leaking tap — these are often 30-minute fixes you can do yourself for under $50. We'll talk you through it on the phone for free. If you'd rather not, we come out for $149."

## On hot water systems

"Heat pumps are worth it if you're replacing an old electric system. The VEU rebate in Victoria covers a chunk of it. We handle the paperwork. Like-for-like gas replacement is faster but a heat pump is cheaper to run."

## On choosing a plumber

"Check the licence number. In Victoria it's on the plumbing register. If a plumber won't give you a fixed price before they start, don't use them. If they quote without seeing the job, they're guessing — and when they find the 'unexpected problem,' it'll cost extra."
