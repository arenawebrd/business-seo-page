# Humour reference — dad-joke frequency, anti-patterns, examples

Dad jokes: 0-2 per page max. They appear in headings or pull quotes, not body copy. If a heading works without a joke, use the version without the joke.

## Anti-patterns

- Don't pun on "pipe," "drain," "flow" unless it's genuinely funny. Most aren't.
- Don't make jokes about disasters. "Your basement floods" is not a punchline.
- Don't use toilet humour in a plumbing site. Too on-the-nose and dates instantly.
- Don't force a joke when the page is about something serious (burst pipes, gas leaks). Serious page, serious tone.

## Examples that work

**"No call-out fee. Seriously — we don't have one."**

Works because it's a real selling point stated plainly, with a small emphasis that reads as dry.

**"Most jobs finished same day. We don't leave until the mess is gone."**

Not a joke. Just honest. Reads as trustworthy.

**"Flat price before we start. Decline, we leave — no call-out fee."**

The "decline, we leave" is Marco being dry about how he does business. It's slightly humorous because it's so blunt, but it's also a real policy.

## Examples that don't work

**"We're flush with expertise."**

Pun on "flush." Don't do this. It's the exact kind of thing Marco avoids.

**"Our team is on the cutting edge of plumbing technology."**

Not a joke, but reads as marketing copy. Marco would say "we've got the parts on the van" instead.
