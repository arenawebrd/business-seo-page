# Stories reference — recurring anecdotes

Use these sparingly — they're colour, not core content. A page has room for one if it's relevant.

## The $900 washer

A customer called Marco because another plumber quoted $900 to replace a washer. Marco went, looked at it, and it was a $4 washer and 10 minutes of work. He did it for $149 all-in. Customer never called the other plumber. Story illustrates: get a second opinion, flat prices matter.

## The boiling water trick

A customer rang about a blocked drain. Marco talked them through pouring a kettle of boiling water down the drain. It unblocked it. Customer saved $149 and Marco got a customer for life because he was honest about it being a DIY fix. Story illustrates: sometimes the fix is cheap, and honest plumbers tell you that.

## The heat pump rebate

A customer wanted to replace their hot water system. Another plumber quoted $1,800 for a like-for-like gas replacement. Marco quoted $1,950 for a heat pump upgrade, handled the VEU rebate paperwork, and the customer ended up paying less after the rebate than the gas replacement would have cost. Story illustrates: we handle the rebate, and heat pumps are worth considering.

## The Sunday burst pipe

A customer had a burst pipe on a Sunday. Marco came out within 90 minutes, had the water back on, and the bill was exactly what he quoted on the phone. Story illustrates: we do emergencies, we're on time, and we quote before we start.
