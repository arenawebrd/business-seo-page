# Voice reference — Marco's writing style

Marco is a 45-year-old plumber who's been running his own business in Melbourne's inner south since 2009.

He writes the way he talks: direct, no fluff, occasionally dry. He doesn't do marketing copy. He does honest tradie talk.

## Voice principles

1. **Say what you mean.** No padding. If a job takes 30 minutes, say 30 minutes, not "a quick and efficient service."
2. **Use plain English.** "flush valve" not "flushing apparatus." "leak" not "fluid egress."
3. **Be specific.** "$149 all-in" not "competitive pricing." "Prahran, South Yarra, St Kilda" not "our service area."
4. **Skip the superlatives.** No "industry-leading," "state-of-the-art," "best in class." If someone else is better, Marco hasn't met them, and he doesn't pretend.
5. **Admit when something's not worth calling about.** "Try a kettle of boiling water first" is better than "call us for a consultation."
6. **Lead with the price when it's a selling point.** Flat price, no call-out fee, upfront — these go near the top.
7. **Write for the person with the problem, not the person selling the solution.** "Running toilet won't stop?" beats "Our toilet repair services."

## Sentence-level rules

- Short sentences. Mostly under 25 words.
- Active voice. "We fix burst pipes" not "burst pipes are fixed by us."
- Contractions are fine. "we'll," "it's," "doesn't."
- "you" and "we" — direct address. Not "the customer" or "the company."
- Paragraphs are 1-3 sentences. White space is a feature.

## Things Marco would never say

- "We are passionate about plumbing"
- "Our state-of-the-art equipment"
- "Industry-leading service"
- "Your trusted plumbing partner"
- "Contact us today for a free quote"

## Things Marco would say

- "Honest plumbers. Same-day if we can."
- "Flat price before we start. Decline, we leave — no call-out fee."
- "Most jobs finished same day. 12-month workmanship warranty."
- "Call now and speak to a real plumber — no call centre, no sales script."
- "We'll tell you what it'll cost before anyone gets in a van."
